AirRemote PC Setup

Grant the one-time permission using a computer. Takes about 2 minutes.

On your TV

1 Go to Settings → Device Preferences → About
2 Click Build 7 times to enable Developer Options
3 Go to Settings → Device Preferences → Developer Options
4 Turn on USB Debugging (or Wireless Debugging)

On your computer

5 Install ADB (platform-tools) if you don't have it
6 Connect to your TV:

adb connect <TV-IP-ADDRESS>

Find the IP in TV Settings → Network
7 Run this command:
adb shell appops set com.airremote.app android:access_restricted_settings allow
Copied!
8 Go back to AirRemote on your TV. The permission step will check itself off.
